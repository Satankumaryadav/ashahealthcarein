Privacy policy

How we handle information on this website.

1. Information we collect

This website is designed to provide clinic and healthcare information only. We do not maintain a patient database, patient registration system, medical records, appointment booking database, or any diagnostic or prescription records.

2. Website analytics and contact form data

We may collect general website usage information and optional contact form details solely to respond to enquiries, improve service quality, and manage communications. Such data is used only for legitimate website and business purposes.

3. No patient data storage

The website does not store patient history, medical diagnoses, treatment plans, prescriptions, or personal healthcare records. The site is informational only and does not provide patient care management, scheduling, or medical record storage.

4. Cookies

Cookies may be used to improve website functionality and measure basic traffic and user experience. Users can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings.

5. Contact and communication

When you contact us through the website, we may use the information supplied to reply to your enquiry and maintain a basic communication record for that purpose only.

6. Third-party services

This website may use third-party hosting or analytics services as part of normal website operations. We encourage users to review the policies of those providers where relevant.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent through the site, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
